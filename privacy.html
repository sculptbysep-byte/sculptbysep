Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

Who we are

SculptBySep is the trading name of Sepehr Mokhberi, ABN 46 808 145 710, a coaching business based in Sydney, New South Wales, Australia. This policy explains how we handle personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

When you complete the coaching application on this site, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and contact number
  • Your answers to the application questions, which cover your training goals, obstacles, how long you have been working towards the goal, and your reasons for applying now

When you browse the site, we and our analytics providers automatically collect technical information including your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring site, and time spent on the site.

If you go on to become a coaching client, we collect further information needed to deliver coaching. That information is covered by the terms of your coaching agreement as well as this policy.

Why we collect it

  • To review your application and decide whether the coaching is a suitable fit
  • To contact you about your application and arrange a call
  • To deliver and administer coaching services if you become a client
  • To measure how the website and our advertising perform, and to improve both

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses Google Tag Manager, which loads Google Analytics, and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel. These services set cookies and similar identifiers that help us understand how visitors find and use the site, and allow us to measure and target advertising on Meta platforms.

Information collected by these tools is processed by Google and Meta under their own privacy policies, and may be transferred to and stored on servers outside Australia, including in the United States.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can manage advertising preferences through the settings offered by Google and Meta. Blocking cookies will not stop you using the site or submitting an application.

Who we share it with

We disclose personal information only to:

  • Service providers who help us run the business, including our website host, email provider, form and analytics providers, and coaching app provider
  • Professional advisers, where reasonably necessary
  • Anyone else where you have consented, or where we are required or authorised by law

Some of these providers are located overseas. Where that is the case, we take reasonable steps to ensure your information is handled consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

How we store and protect it

Personal information is held in secured, access-controlled systems operated by us and our service providers. We take reasonable steps to protect it from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access, modification or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We keep application information for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, and afterwards where we are required to retain it by law. When it is no longer needed, we destroy or de-identify it.

Accessing or correcting your information

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing sculptbysep@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable period. In limited circumstances we may refuse a request, and if we do we will tell you why in writing.

You can also ask us to stop sending you marketing communications at any time, by using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.

Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first at sculptbysep@gmail.com so we can try to resolve it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version published on this page is the current one, and the date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact
