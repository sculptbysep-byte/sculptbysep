Founder story

Why I built SculptBySep

I didn't originally plan on working in the fitness industry. My background was in science and I completed a Bachelor of Science majoring in Chemistry, then moved into cyber security sales, working alongside entrepreneurial CEOs and high-performing professionals.

From the outside, things looked good, but personally I felt disconnected from myself physically. That eventually led me to make a big career change in 2016 and pursue personal training full-time.

Over the years, I naturally gravitated towards coaching ambitious men because I understood the pressures firsthand. Long work hours, stress, inconsistent routines, travel, constantly pushing professionally whilst physically putting yourself last.

In 2022 I stepped on stage for my first Men's Physique competition whilst running my business full-time. That experience reinforced something I strongly believe now. You can build a great physique, perform at a high level professionally, and still have balance in your life. But it takes discipline, structure, and the right systems.

SculptBySep was built to help ambitious men create structure, build confidence, improve their health and physique, and develop standards they can actually maintain over the long term.

Common questions

Before you apply.

Is this fully online?

Online coaching is fully remote, delivered worldwide through the custom SculptBySep app. 1-to-1 Personal Training is in person, based in Sydney.

Do I need gym access?

A standard commercial gym is ideal, but programming can be built around a home setup or hotel gym where needed.

Is nutrition included?

Yes. Nutrition coaching and macro guidance are included, built around your schedule, preferences and training load.

How often do we check in?

Weekly. Check-ins cover training, nutrition, recovery and lifestyle, and your programme is adjusted from there.

What if I travel often?

Travel is planned for, not worked around after the fact. The system is built to hold through travel and heavy work weeks.

How long does it take to see results?

Most clients see meaningful change within the first 8 to 12 weeks when they execute consistently.

What does it cost?

Pricing depends on the pathway and session frequency, and is shared after your application and strategy call. There is no payment to apply.
