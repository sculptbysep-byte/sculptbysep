01What in-person personal training actually gives you

Face to face coaching delivers something online cannot fully replicate. Live technique correction, real-time intensity management, and the social contract of someone standing in front of you twice a week.

It is the best option for beginners, anyone returning from injury, and clients who genuinely struggle to train without external accountability.

It is also the most expensive and least flexible model. You are tied to a location and a schedule.

02Where online coaching wins

Online coaching is built for men who can already train, but need structure, programming and accountability. It removes the scheduling problem entirely. You train when it fits, where it fits, with full coach support behind the scenes.

A high quality online program includes personalised training, nutrition coaching, weekly check-ins, video form reviews, and direct messaging access to your coach. It is not a PDF. It is a system.

For busy professionals, business owners and frequent travellers, online coaching almost always outperforms in-person, simply because consistency beats intensity.

03When to choose each model

Choose in-person if you are a beginner, you need hands-on technique work, or you genuinely will not train without a coach in front of you.

Choose online coaching if you can train independently, your schedule is unpredictable, you travel often, or you want world-class programming without being tied to a gym in one city.

Some clients do both. Start in-person for three to six months, then transition online once habits and technique are bulletproof.

04The quality question

The model matters less than the coach. A great online program is more transformational than mediocre in-person sessions. A great in-person coach beats a generic online template every time.

What matters is whether your training is personalised, your nutrition is structured, your progress is tracked, and someone qualified is reviewing the data each week.
