Choosing The Right Online Coach: 7 Things To Look For Before You Commit

Choose the wrong coach and it becomes the most expensive PDF you have ever bought. Seven things to check first.

01Real coaching, not a template

Many online coaches sell templates with a name on them. The program you receive is the same program a thousand other clients received this month.

A real online coach builds your program around your goal, training history, equipment, injuries, schedule and stress load. If you cannot see your personal context in your plan, it is not coaching.

02A proper coaching app

PDFs and spreadsheets are not modern coaching tools. Look for a custom app that delivers your training, nutrition targets, check-ins, progress tracking and messaging in one place.

A great app removes friction. You open it, you train, you log, you eat, you check in. That simplicity is what drives consistency.

03Weekly check-ins with real adjustments

A weekly check-in should review your data, photos, training, nutrition and life context, then translate that into specific adjustments. If your check-in is a one line emoji response, your coaching is not coaching.

04Direct access to your actual coach

Some companies sell access to a coach, then route your messages through an assistant. Confirm exactly who is reviewing your training, programming your nutrition, and responding to your questions.

You want one qualified person who knows your file, your goals, and your patterns.

05Track record with people like you

Look for transformations from clients with a life similar to yours. Busy professionals, parents, travellers, business owners. A coach who only transforms full-time fitness enthusiasts is not the right fit for a man running a company.

06Honest conversations on the first call

A good coach tells you what your goal will realistically require, in time, effort and lifestyle change. If the first call sounds like a sales script with guaranteed timelines, it is a sales script.

07A clear standard

The right coach has a defined philosophy and refuses to compromise on it. Evidence over trends. Sustainability over extremes. Consistency over perfection. You should be able to hear it in the way they speak about training and clients.
